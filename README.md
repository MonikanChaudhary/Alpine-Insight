# Alpine-Insight
# 1. Project Title / Headline

# Alpine Insights – Global Ski Resort Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to explore, analyze, and compare ski resorts across different countries and continents.

# 2. Short Description / Purpose

**Alpine Insights** is a data analytics and visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides an overview of the global ski resort landscape by analyzing resort distribution, pricing, slope difficulty, lift infrastructure, and available facilities.

The primary purpose of this dashboard is to transform raw ski resort data into meaningful visual insights, helping users understand differences between ski destinations, compare resort characteristics, and explore pricing and infrastructure patterns.

The report consists of three interactive pages:
- Global Overview
- Terrain & Lift Analysis
- Pricing & Resort Comparison

# 3. Tech Stack

The following tools and technologies were used:

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Dashboard development and interactive data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Creating calculated measures and KPIs.
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV:** Dataset handling and preliminary data inspection.

# 4. Data Source

The project uses a ski resort dataset containing information about approximately 499 ski resorts across 38 countries.

The dataset includes attributes such as:

- Resort name and geographical location.
- Country and continent.
- Resort pricing and ski season.
- Beginner, intermediate, and difficult slopes.
- Total slopes and longest ski run.
- Surface lifts, chair lifts, gondola lifts, and total lift capacity.
- Snow cannons, snowparks, and other resort facilities.
- Child-friendly facilities, night skiing, and summer skiing availability.

The dataset was used for data preparation, exploratory analysis, KPI creation, and dashboard development.

**Dataset Source:** [(https://mavenanalytics.io/data-playground/ski-resorts)]

# 5. Features / Highlights

## A. Business Problem

Ski resort information is spread across multiple attributes, making it difficult to compare destinations based on pricing, terrain variety, infrastructure, and available facilities.

Without a consolidated analytical view, users may struggle to identify differences between resorts, understand pricing patterns, and evaluate the infrastructure offered by different ski destinations.

## B. Goal of the Dashboard

The objective of Alpine Insights is to develop an interactive business intelligence dashboard that:

- Provides a consolidated overview of global ski resorts.
- Analyzes resort distribution across countries and continents.
- Compares slope difficulty and terrain availability.
- Evaluates lift infrastructure and resort capacity.
- Explores the relationship between resort pricing and total slope availability.
- Helps users identify patterns, differences, and unusual resort characteristics.

## C. Walkthrough of Key Visuals

### Page 1 – Global Overview

Provides a high-level summary of the global ski resort landscape.

**Key Visuals:**
- KPI Cards: Total Resorts, Countries Covered, Total Lifts, Average Price, Child-Friendly Resorts, and Total Slopes.
- Bar Chart: Distribution of ski resorts across countries.
- Donut Chart: Distribution of slopes by difficulty level.

**Purpose:** To understand the overall distribution, scale, and characteristics of ski resorts worldwide.

### Page 2 – Terrain & Lift Analysis

Focuses on the terrain characteristics and infrastructure available at ski resorts.

**Key Visuals:**
- KPI Cards: Average Lifts per Resort, Average Slopes per Resort, Average Longest Run, and facility availability indicators.
- Stacked Bar Chart: Slope difficulty distribution by continent.
- Scatter Chart: Relationship between total slopes and lift infrastructure.
- Column Chart: Average longest run by continent.
- Stacked Column Chart: Lift infrastructure comparison across continents.

**Purpose:** To evaluate terrain variety, resort capacity, and differences in lift infrastructure across ski destinations.

### Page 3 – Pricing & Resort Comparison

Explores resort pricing patterns and compares individual resorts.

**Key Visuals:**
- Bar Chart: Average resort price by country.
- Column Chart: Average resort price by continent.
- Scatter Chart: Resort Price vs Total Slopes.
- Comparison Table: Resort-level comparison using price, total slopes, total lifts, and longest run.

**Purpose:** To explore pricing variations, understand the relationship between resort size and price, and compare individual ski destinations.

## D. Business Impact

The dashboard demonstrates how raw data can be transformed into an interactive analytical solution that supports:

- **Comparative Analysis:** Makes it easier to compare ski resorts across countries and continents.
- **Pricing Analysis:** Helps explore pricing differences and identify resorts with different price-to-terrain profiles.
- **Infrastructure Evaluation:** Provides insights into slope availability, lift infrastructure, and resort capacity.
- **Data-Driven Exploration:** Enables users to investigate resort characteristics through interactive filters and visualizations.
- **Decision Support:** Provides a consolidated view that can support preliminary ski destination research and comparison.

## E. Key Insights & Analytical Opportunities

The dashboard enables users to investigate questions such as:

- Which countries have the highest number of ski resorts?
- How are ski slopes distributed across beginner, intermediate, and difficult categories?
- Which continents have greater slope and lift infrastructure?
- How does the average longest ski run vary across continents?
- Do resorts with more slopes generally have higher prices?
- Are there resorts with relatively high prices despite having fewer slopes?
- How does resort infrastructure vary between individual destinations?
